We check the fit before you pay

Send your chassis number, like GRJ150-0123456, or a 17-character VIN. It's on your registration card. Parts from Dar stock and Kariakoo shops can be returned within 7 days if they don't fit. Imported parts are ordered for your car, so we only take them back if we sent the wrong one.
